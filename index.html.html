FlowSync CRM

A custom CRM that automates customer and job management, so nobody retypes the same details twice.

Result: less manual data entry across the team's workflow.

  • Creates and updates customer profiles automatically from Google Sheets.
  • Makes a Drive folder for each customer and files uploads in the right place.
  • Shows submission status, email history and required documents on one profile.
  • Sends customer emails through Gmail without leaving the CRM.
  • Live dashboard for job progress, submission status and pending tasks.
